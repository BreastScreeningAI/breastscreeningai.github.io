Reporting boundary

What this page does and does not claim

This page summarizes public materials already disclosed in the repository. It does not add private data, unpublished site-specific outcomes, patient information, regulatory claims or new financial statements. Reported figures remain tied to their original study or pilot context, and larger prospective validation is still needed.
